Ideas to Implement

1. **Deliver Immediate Value**
  
  * Offer something useful right away to visitors.
    
  * This aligns with the _first law of persuasion_: people respond when they gain value upfront.
    
2. **Interactive, Low-Friction Forms**
  
  * Start with a single engaging question about their brand or interests.
    
  * Reveal additional questions one by one to reduce friction and make form-filling feel natural.
    
3. **Bold Positioning Statements**
  
  * Use strong, aspirational phrases such as:
    
    * _“For those who refuse to be ignored.”_
      
    * _“Design for the top 1% of the market.”_
      
4. **Scarcity & Urgency**
  
  * Create urgency by showing limited availability (e.g., “Only 3 spots left this month”).
    
  * Highlight the cost of inaction: _“Every day without a professional site costs you customers.”_
    
5. **Industry Expertise**
  
  * Demonstrate deep understanding of their industry.
    
  * Use language and examples that resonate with their specific challenges.
    
6. **Loss-Framed Messaging**
  
  * Frame copy around what clients are _losing_ by not upgrading.
    
  * Example: Instead of _“Get a better website,”_ say _“Stop losing potential customers to a slow, outdated user experience.”_
    
  * Support with real-world statistics and data to make the message credible.
