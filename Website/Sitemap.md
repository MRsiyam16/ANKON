### **1. Home Page**

* **Hero Section**
  
  * Minimal design with a background video
    
  * Strong headline and subheadline
    
  * Clear primary call-to-action (CTA)
    
* **Social Proof**
  
  * Logos of clients you’ve worked with
    
* **Problem & Solution**
  
  * Highlight what clients are losing/missing out on
    
  * Show how your services directly fix these issues
    
* **Portfolio Showcase**
  
  * Display selected work for other brands
    
  * Case studies or visual highlights
    
* **Client Growth (Optional)**
  
  * Section showing how clients have grown
    
  * If no data yet, keep it conceptual or use placeholder visuals
    
* **Services Overview**
  
  * Concise list of your core services
    
  * Icons or short descriptions for clarity
    
* **Testimonials**
  
  * Reviews form prevoius clients
    
* **Call to Action**
  
  * Strong CTA with a form (e.g., “Work with us” or “Get started”)
    
  * Keep it simple and easy to complete
    
* **Footer
