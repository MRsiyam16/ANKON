# ANKON Brand Identity Guidelines
**Version 1.1** | *Refined for Minimalism*

---

## 1. Brand Core
ANKON is a design agency that merges the precision of modern minimalism with the soulful roots of artistic sketching (Ankon). We believe in "Functional Luxury"—design that is as useful as it is beautiful.

### Vision
To be the global benchmark for minimalist design that respects cultural heritage while pushing digital boundaries.

### Mission
To strip away the noise and deliver clarity. We transform complex brand challenges into elegant, intuitive, and high-impact visual systems.

---

## 2. Visual Identity

### The Logo
The ANKON logo is a masterclass in geometric minimalism. @ANKON logo.png
- **The Wordmark:** Set in **Montserrat**, the characters are spaced generously to convey a sense of premium luxury.
- **The Stylized 'A':** The central mark, an 'A' without a crossbar, represents a drafting compass, a mountain peak, and an open gateway to creativity.

### Typography
The brand is built on the **Montserrat** typeface system to ensure a clean, geometric, and modern feel across all touchpoints.

* **Primary Headings:** Montserrat light (All Caps)
    * *Letter Spacing:* +200 to +400 (Tracking)
* **Subheadings:** Montserrat regular (All Caps)
    * *Letter Spacing:* +150
* **Body Copy:** Montserrat Light or Regular
    * *Line Height:* 1.6em for readability.

### Color Palette
We utilize a high-contrast, "paper and ink" inspired palette to create a timeless aesthetic.

| Color Name | HEX | RGB | Use Case |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Obsidian Black** | `#121212` | (18, 18, 18) | Logos, Headings, Dark UI |
| **Agency Cream** | `#F5F1E3` | (245, 241, 227) | Backgrounds, Stationery |
| **Pure Light** | `#FFFFFF` | (255, 255, 255) | High-contrast accents |
| **Slate Grey** | `#707070` | (112, 112, 112) | Secondary text, Lines |

## 3. Layout & Composition
ANKON layouts should feel like an art gallery:
- **Negative Space:** Use "white space" (or cream space) aggressively. It is our most important design element.
- **Grids:** Use a strict 12-column grid for digital and a 4-column grid for print.
- **Imagery:** Photography should be high-contrast, featuring natural textures (linen, paper, concrete) and soft, cinematic lighting.

---

## 4. Brand Voice
How ANKON communicates:
- **Concise:** We say more with fewer words.
- **Authoritative:** We speak with the confidence of experts.
- **Atmospheric:** We use evocative language that appeals to the senses.

**Example Tone Shift:**
* *Instead of:* "We make logos and websites for your business."
* *Use:* "We architect visual identities that resonate with clarity and purpose."

---

## 5. Usage Constraints (The "Don'ts")
- **Don't** use gradients or drop shadows on the logo.
- **Don't** use bright, neon, or primary colors (e.g., bright red or blue).
- **Don't** use decorative or "handwritten" fonts; stay strictly within the Montserrat family.
- **Don't** crowd the logo; maintain a clear space equal to the height of the 'N' on all sides.
